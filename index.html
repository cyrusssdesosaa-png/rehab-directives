Care from our clinic at Mission Pines.

The team at Rehab Directives uses current treatment to help you get back to a healthy life as soon as it is safe to do so.

The clinic is a working therapy gym inside Mission Pines. Patients come for hands-on care, not for a linked portal or an online chart. Call the office and the team will tell you what to bring.

Our goal

Our goal is to help you feel and perform at your best. We want every patient to move with less pain, and to get stronger so the same problem is less likely to come back.

The mission is to help you return to work and to the daily activities you had before. A licensed team builds the plan, then stays with you until those goals are met safely.

Physical therapy

Strength, walking, balance, and movement after illness, surgery, or injury. Care stays with the adult plan your doctor and therapist agree on.

Occupational therapy

Practice for the tasks of daily living, so getting dressed, moving through the day, and using your arms again can happen at the clinic before it has to happen at home.

Speech therapy

Speech therapy is part of care here, together with physical and occupational therapy, when communication or swallowing is part of the recovery.

The clinic

These are the rooms at Mission Pines. Therapy happens on the gym floor. Families can sit with patients in the dining room down the hall.

Insurance

MedicareA prescription from a doctor is required.
Nevada MedicaidAsk the office to check the plan before the first visit.

Each plan is different. Copays, deductibles, and therapy benefits are not the same from one patient to the next. The office checks benefits and eligibility before care starts, and bills insurance as a courtesy.

Call for the details of your own plan. The team will help with questions about coverage.

If a medical plan does not cover the visit, cash-pay fees are available. The cost of the first evaluation and later visits depends on the services the therapist provides.
